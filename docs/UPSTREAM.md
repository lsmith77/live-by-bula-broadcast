# Upstream asks

The features these overlays want from UltiOrganizer and Live! by BULA, in one place. This is a digest for an upstream reader: each entry says what is asked, what it unlocks and roughly what it costs, and links to the full case. The arguments live with the features that produced them in [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) and [`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) and are deliberately not repeated here, so this page cannot drift far from them. Where an overlay-side workaround exists the entry names it — each one is evidence the feature was wanted enough to build a bridge, and each one retires when the upstream feature lands. When opening an upstream issue, paste the entry and link the full case.

Everything below was established against real payloads and the UO schema while building the overlays, not guessed. Gaps that a config toggle or a directory of images already covers are not asks and are not listed — [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.1 and the settings table in [`README.md`](README.md) cover those.

**Last assessed:** 2026-08-29

## If only one thing changed

**Record possession changes.** It alone unlocks the complete break-chance indicator, clean holds, turnover counts and offensive efficiency, and it is the only ask that cannot be derived, configured or worked around from the overlay side ([`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.8).

## Asks against UltiOrganizer

1. **Record possession changes in the Scorekeeper** — a timestamped record of each change of possession within a point. Team-only capture (one button) unlocks break chance, clean holds, turnover counts and offensive efficiency; player-attributed capture adds the offensive counterpart to blocks, and is a separate, harder ask. `uo_player_stats` already carries the columns (`offence_turns`, `defence_turns`, `breaks`, …) and writes them as hard-coded zeros, so this completes scaffolding rather than introducing a concept — but it needs a capture policy (next item) from the start. Worked around today by an operator- or commentator-declared possession log, kept deliberately ephemeral and not exportable so the bridge cannot become the permanent home. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.4.
2. **A per-event capture policy** — an `EVENT_SETTING` declaring what an event records (blocks, possession, matchings), cascading from event to pool to game. The per-game override is the important part — a staffed Sunday final can record more than Friday pool play on six fields — and the same declaration should drive the Scorekeeper's input surface, so each game's sheet shows exactly the inputs its policy declares. Consumers can then tell a zero from not tracked, which every adaptive graphic depends on; blocks are the cautionary example of shipping data without one. Worked around today by a heuristic: a game with zero blocks across every player is treated as not captured, and the column is dropped rather than rendered as zeros. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §4 and §10.2.
3. **Record gender matching (FMP/MMP) on the roster** — a per-event competition designation for each player, plus a structured division type, recorded in the sport's own terms and never derived from `uo_player_profile.gender`. Unlocks gender-balanced top-scorer cards (already built overlay-side, activating the moment the field appears) and FMP/MMP labels wherever players are listed — without it, mixed-division coverage silently erases the FMP half of the game. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.5.
4. **Record the first point's gender ratio, per mixed game** — it is circled on the paper scoresheet and never captured, so the ABBA pattern is derivable but its labels are not, and per-ratio scoring splits cannot be aggregated across games. One column would make every mixed game ever played analysable by ratio. Worked around today by asking at the desk: the commentator declares point 1's ratio once per game, stored with the game for that broadcast alone. Full case: [`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) §6b and §6c.
5. **Pronunciation hints and pronouns on the player profile** — short self-declared free-text fields on `uo_player_profile`, optional, never inferred, published only through the existing per-field `public` opt-in. Cheap because the player-owned edit surface and the consent mechanism already exist. Worked around today by structured fields on the commentator desk's notes store, filled through the CSV round trip where each player writes their own row — self-declared, but expiring with the desk's room; the profile is the destination, because the answer should follow the player between tournaments. Full case: [`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) §5.
6. **Structured commentary fields on the player profile** — short self-declared lines beside `story` and `achievements`: other sports played, how they came to ultimate, occupation or study, interests, previous clubs. Same home, edit surface and opt-in as the previous item. Worked around today by the CSV round trip ([`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) §5a) — each player writes their own row, which is the right authorship — and by a commentator-authored notebook; both expire with the desk's room and are gathered again for every tournament, where this asks for the player's own words following them between tournaments. Full case: [`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) §5.
7. **Smaller schema gaps**, each blocking one specific graphic: an explicit halftime event (distinct from the `half_cap` call), a soft/hard cap distinction, a team colour on `uo_team`, Timekeeper cap templates actually bound to a game, and who-is-on-this-point line data. Two are already worked around at the desk — team colour by an operator-prepared kit palette, line data by the commentator page's shared line selection — and the others have no workaround. Full case: the table in [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.2.
8. **A per-tournament logo on `uo_season`** — every existing logo path is installation-wide, which works only while one Live! deployment serves one event; a combined tournament-plus-federation lockup is inherently specific to one event, so the event is where it belongs. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.6.
9. **Identifiers for people and clubs that survive leaving one installation** — so a career, a club history and a head-to-head record can span instances instead of ending at the edge of whichever database hosted the event. The identifier must be issued rather than derived (WFDF player numbers are the real-world anchor), clubs are the safer first half, and consent and erasure do not federate — which argues for federating by reference rather than by copy. By far the largest ask: a UO-wide change to identity, privacy and the API before it is an overlay feature. Full case: [`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) §8a.

## Asks against Live! by BULA

1. **Per-game block counts in the game payload** — they exist in `uo_defense` and via `GameTeamDefenseBoard()`, but `GameManager` never exposes them; `entity=teams` carries tournament totals only, so a "blocks in this game" card cannot be built at all. The contemplated workaround — an overlay-side endpoint reading `uo_defense` directly — is deliberately held open, because it would relax the project's no-direct-database rule. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §3.1 and §3.4.
2. **Expose `uo_player_profile` in a payload** — `entity=players&id=<id>` returns `Invalid ID` even for ids the list endpoint itself returned, so nickname, position, story and the rest are unreachable despite already having a per-field `public` opt-in. This may be a bug rather than a decision, and it is the prerequisite for both profile asks above. Full case: [`COMMENTATOR.md`](COMMENTATOR.md) §5.
3. **Player photos** — Live! has no player image field anywhere; `TEAM_PHOTOS_ENABLED` covers team photos only. The planned workaround is an overlay-local `players/` directory mirroring the existing team-logo store, which can only ever serve one installation; an image field in Live! would serve every deployment. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §5 and §10.1.

## Considered and excluded

**Picks, fouls, travels and other calls** — genuinely novel overlays, and the clearest case of capture cost exceeding value: frequent, ambiguous, sometimes contested and retracted, and a full-time job to record accurately. Out of scope unless a tournament staffs a dedicated stats keeper. Full case: [`STUDIO.md`](STUDIO.md) §10.7.
